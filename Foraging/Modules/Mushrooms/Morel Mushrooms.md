## Rare, and delicious as all hell. They can't be grown! They have a flavor unlike any other mushroom, they're one of my personal favorites. Carry in a breathable container to spread their spores.

IDENTIFICATION: Hollow. Cream colored stem, yellowish brownish cap with sponge like texture. Take a spore print by cutting it in half, leaving cut side down on paper, let sit overnight and find a yellowish white spore print in the morning!
Find beneath dead and dying Elm Trees and Tulip Trees and old Apple Trees, and various other areas.
THEY HIDE REALLY WELL! LOOK THOROUGHLY.
Once you find one, you're likely to find more in the nearby area :)
Put on your morel goggles homies

Store in an open container or paper bag in the fridge. Good for a week.
Don't wash. Brush to clean.
