## 

IDENTIFICATION: Green woody [[Perennial Plant]] vine that is dotted with sharp thorns. [[Simple Leaves]] have smooth edges, and grow in an [[Alternate Growth Habit]]. Leaves are 2 in wide, round, glossy, and green. Lighter green in the spring time when it's time to harvest.  Edible shoots have curling tendrils too. 

Grows as an understory plant in dense forests.
