## Native, there's kind of a hype around them so people commercialize it. Don't forage too much, spread the seeds (image 2) and transplant some of them.

AKA wild leeks

IDENTIFICATION: 2-3 long oval shaped leaves that are 1-2 inches wide. Leaves are a bright spring green with light striping. Bulb is bright white like a green onion. Bulb can be found with a light brown sheath that feels papery. Smell like onion/garlic when crushed!
Find in [[Deciduous Forest]]. Like to grow around sugar maples.
Find in [[Floodplain]]s of mountain streams. Enjoy damp soil.
Find in the spring!
