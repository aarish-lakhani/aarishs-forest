## Invasive, harvest as much as you'd like (and more to promote native species, please!)

IDENTIFICATION: Grows in dense clumps, [[Monocot]]. Long, slender, and round leaves, similar to chives or green onion. Smells like garlic/onion.
Found in [[Deciduous Forest]]. 
Wooded parks, community gardens, etc.
One of the first plants to emerge in the early spring.
Dig up the bulbs when harvesting (they taste good too). Just be careful not to disturb the rest of the soil!
