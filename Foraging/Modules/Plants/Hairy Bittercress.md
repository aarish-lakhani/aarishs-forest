## Exotic invasive, harvest to your content!

IDENTIFICATION: [[Rosette]], round leaves like baby ferns. In the spring, there are central flower stalks (see white flower in image 2). Flowers from 4-10 inches tall. 
Find in [[Disturbed Area]]. Hiking trails, gardens, etc. Lawns not sprayed with herbicides.
Find in late fall to early spring.
Sautee or eat at a salad green. Similar to Arugula. Can blanche and freeze for later, but better nutrient wise fresh.
Bit of a pain to process. Because of [[Rosette]] shape, holds onto dirt and sand. Wash thoroughly.
