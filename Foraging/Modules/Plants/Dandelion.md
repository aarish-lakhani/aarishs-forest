## You've seen this before. You can eat it! HIGHLY INVASIVE, HARVEST TO YOUR HEART'S CONTENT!

IDENTIFICATION: You'll find it.
Find in hillside or local park. Hiking trail. Don't collect anywhere soil might be contaminated by heavy metals. 

CAUTION: Herbicides sprayed on dandelion in some urban areas. You don't need to worry about that if the plant is alive. It's probably fine. Give it an extra rinse.

Greens: greens taste good in early spring, sauteed or on pizza. Before dandelions flower.
Flowers: remove dark green thingies on the back of the flower (bitter) and eat the mildly sweet flowers! Or steep 'em in cream and make it into a dandelion ice cream.
Roots: dried, ground up, and used as a tea substitute! non-caffeinated, but a good warm beverage. Harvest roots in the fall.
